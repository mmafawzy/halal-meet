# 02 — Engagement, Retention & Monetization

← [Back to index](README.md)

> **Strategy in one line:** People come back because there's always someone new and compatible to discover, plus something *meaningful* waiting (Top Picks, progress, reveals, family), and they pay because paying gets them *closer to marriage faster*. We never use tricks that exploit them; in this market, trust is the product.

---

## 1. Engagement architecture: five loops

| Loop | Frequency | Trigger | Action | Reward | Investment (makes the next loop better) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Discover feed** | Any time (multiple sessions/day) | "27 new compatible profiles", free interests reset at midnight | Browse the infinite compatibility-ranked feed | Explained, compatible people, always more | Every interest/pass re-ranks the feed in real time |
| **Top Picks** | Daily | Top Picks refresh at chosen time (after Maghrib default) | Review 3–10 best mutual matches | The highest-quality profiles of the day | Feedback sharpens tomorrow's picks |
| **Daily Question** | Daily | "Today's question" card | Answer 1 question (10 s) | See how matches answered + reveal progress | Better compatibility data |
| **Conversation progress** | Per message | Reply / topic card | Answer topics together | Topic coverage ↑, stage advance celebrations | Deeper connection, Discuss flags resolved |
| **Family loop** | Weekly / event-based | Guardian digest, approval requests | Guardian reviews, approves, opines | Family support, faster progression | Guardians bring new users (siblings, cousins) |
| **Insight loop** | Weekly (Friday) | Weekly Insight | Read insight, act on suggestions | Self-knowledge, curiosity | More questions answered, profile improvements |

**Plus milestone moments** (variable, high emotion): mutual match, Serious Interest received, stage completion, families introduced, first meeting, engagement. Each has a dedicated celebration screen and an optional share card.

## 2. The Discover feed: the core habit

**Why it works:** there's always something new to see, so users never hit a wall and leave. Unlike typical feeds, ours is ordered by **mutual compatibility** and explains every card, so the time users spend browsing turns into real matches and conversations.

Mechanics:
- **Infinite, compatibility-ranked** vertical feed (see 01 §21, 04 §4). The strongest matches come first; new members are injected as they join.
- **Freshness hooks:** a "New since your last visit" counter, new-member highlights, and real-time re-ranking after each interest/pass, so every session feels different.
- **Daily interest allowance:** Free 10/day (resets at local midnight), Plus/Gold unlimited. Browsing is always unlimited; the reset gives free users a daily reason to return, and the limit is the primary Plus upgrade driver.
- **Saved profiles:** when out of interests, users bookmark profiles, which creates a built-in reason to come back tomorrow.
- **Top Picks** (secondary daily appointment): Free 3 · Plus 6 · Gold 10 + 1 Curated Pick · Elite: matchmaker-curated. Refreshes at the user's chosen time (default 30 min after local Maghrib; Ramadan: after Taraweeh). Teaser push: *"Your Top Picks are ready — one is 90%+ compatible."* (only when true).
- **Quality transparency:** below the market quality floor, the feed shows a divider and labels the rest "Broader matches"; dealbreakers are never violated. This keeps trust high even in an endless feed.
- **Return reward:** the first session after 3+ days away opens with a "Welcome back" set of the best new profiles since the last visit.
- **Mindful browsing:** after ~60 cards with no interest, a card suggests adjusting preferences, which catches bad settings before they cause churn.

## 3. Progress systems (gamification without cheapening)

### 3.1 Profile Strength
Basic → Good → Strong → **Excellent ✦**. Each level visibly improves distribution (e.g. Strong+ profiles are eligible for Top Picks and Curated Picks). Badge shown only to the user, not ranked publicly.

### 3.2 Journey Bar (per connection)
Stage progression with celebrations. Shows **topic coverage x/15**. The "almost there" effect (*"3 topics left before you're ready to involve families"*) drives conversation depth.

### 3.3 Compatibility Reveals
Dimensions stay locked until both users answer enough questions in that category. *"Answer 4 Finance questions to reveal your Finance compatibility with 6 matches."* It's free, curiosity-driven, and improves data quality, which is the best kind of engagement loop.

### 3.4 Trust badges (public, factual only)
✓ Identity · ✓ Phone · ✓ Profession · ✓ Education · ✓ Family Connected · **Respectful Responder** (replies to or respectfully closes ≥ 90% of matches; anti-ghosting reputation).
**No public "piety" badges, no popularity counters, no attractiveness scores.**

### 3.5 Consistency (not streaks)
We avoid loss-based streaks (anxiety-inducing). Instead: *"You answered 18 daily questions this month"* (cumulative, never resets to zero). Monthly milestone at 20 answers = 5 bonus interests + 1 extra Top Pick.

### 3.6 Seasonal programmes
- **Ramadan Mode:** UI theme, reflection questions, Top Picks refreshing at Iftar, reduced notification volume during fasting hours, "Ramadan intentions" profile prompt, charity tie-in (e.g. a donation per engagement).
- **Eid:** Eid greeting cards to matches (templated, respectful), Eid events.
- **"New Year, New Chapter"** (Hijri New Year) re-engagement campaign.
- **Summer Homecoming** (June–August): diaspora users visiting Egypt/Levant/Maghreb set Travel mode dates and locals see *"Visiting Cairo 1–20 Aug"*, which drives Passport sales and urgency to connect before the trip.

## 4. Anti-ghosting system (a retention feature)

Ghosting is the #1 reason users quit dating apps. Fixing it keeps users.
1. "Your move" inbox ordering.
2. Reply nudge at 48 h: *"Sara is waiting for your reply. A short message or a respectful close are both kind."*
3. Match expiry: 7 days without a first message → closes with a neutral card.
4. Auto respectful close after 10 days of one-sided silence.
5. **Respectful Responder** badge + a small ranking boost for responsive users (reciprocity lifts everyone).
6. Users with chronic ghosting see fewer introductions until behaviour improves (soft, explained).

## 5. Notification system

**Principle:** every push must contain real value. A budget prevents spam.

| Category | Examples | Default | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover & Top Picks | "Your Top Picks are ready", "12 new compatible profiles joined near you", "Your 10 interests have refreshed" | On | 1/day combined |
| Match & interest | "It's a match with Sara", "⭐ Someone is seriously interested" | On | Real-time, bundled after 3/hour |
| Messages | "Sara replied" | On | Real-time; collapsed per thread |
| Family | "Your father approved Family Introduction", guardian digest | On | Event-based / weekly |
| Progress | "3 topics left before Stage 5" | On | 2/week |
| Insight | Weekly Insight (Friday) | On | 1/week |
| Re-engagement | "You have 2 matches waiting" (after 3 days inactive) | On | Max 3 over 14 days, then stop |
| Marketing / offers | Ramadan offer, events | **Off unless opted in** | 2/month |

- **Prayer-time quiet windows** (optional): no non-urgent pushes from Adhan to +20 min.
- **Quiet hours** default 23:00–08:00 local.
- **Global budget:** max 5 non-message pushes/day.
- Channels: push, in-app, email digest, WhatsApp (guardians and OTP only; opt-in).
- Every push deep-links to the exact screen.

## 9. Ethical guardrails: banned dark patterns

These rules protect the brand, comply with consumer-protection rules (FTC, EU DSA/UCPD, UK CMA, app-store guidelines), and survive Shariah Advisory Board review.

**We never:**
1. Show fake or bot profiles, fake "someone liked you" alerts, or fake scarcity/countdowns.
2. Sell boosts that rank someone above more-compatible people ("pay to be seen by attractive users").
3. Rank or price based on attractiveness, or reveal attractiveness scores.
4. Hide existing mutual matches or messages behind a paywall. **Replying is always free.**
5. Paywall safety, reporting, blocking, verification or data deletion.
6. Make cancellation harder than signup (two taps, in-app, plus clear store links).
7. Auto-renew without a clear pre-renewal reminder for trials.
8. Use loss-based streaks, loot-box mechanics, or randomised paid rewards.
9. Send re-engagement pushes indefinitely (max 3 over 14 days, then stop).
10. Sell or share personal, religious, conversation or location data for advertising.
11. Withhold Serious Interest from free users (it's always revealed).
12. Penalise users for leaving because they got engaged; we celebrate graduation.

**Engagement health metrics (tracked alongside revenue):**
- **Feed-to-conversation ratio:** cards viewed per conversation started (rising = people browsing without connecting, so fix ranking or preferences rather than adding more content).
- Share of users hitting the "mindful browsing" card (high = preference/ranking problems).
- Session length distribution (a long tail of very long feed-only sessions triggers a design review for compulsive patterns).
- Ghosting rate, respectful-close rate, reports per 1k conversations.
- Refund rate and cancellation reasons.

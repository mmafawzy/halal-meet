## 1. Strategy: global launch, density-first execution

Halal Meet launches **globally on day one**: sign-up is open in every target country, with **Passport live at launch**. A global launch maximises press, word of mouth across diaspora networks, and cross-border Passport revenue. Its main risk is **thin local pools** (users scrolling to the end of their feed in the first session), so the launch is built around four density mechanics:

| Mechanic | How it works |
|---|---|
| **1. Hub cities** | Marketing spend and events concentrate on 16 hub cities (below) where we aim for dense local pools in the first 90 days. Everywhere else is open but grows organically. |
| **2. Country status: Growing → Open** | Each country starts as **Growing**. While Growing, free users automatically see their whole **home region** (e.g. GCC, Maghreb, Western Europe, North America), not just their country, so no one hits an empty feed. A country flips to **Open** (country-level default scope, regional view requires Passport) once it reaches **2,000 verified profiles with ≤ 60/40 gender balance**. |
| **3. Founding Members** | The first 25,000 ID-verified users worldwide get **3 months of Plus + Passport free** and a private Founding Member badge. This seeds cross-border activity and creates a large cohort that converts to paid when the period ends. |
| **4. Gender-balanced onboarding queue** | In any country where the active gender ratio exceeds 65/35, new sign-ups from the over-represented gender complete their profile and verification, then join a short waitlist (shown with an honest position and ETA) while acquisition targets the under-represented side. |

### Regions (for Growing-country pools)
| Region | Countries |
|---|---|
| Egypt & Levant | Egypt, Jordan, (Lebanon, Palestine, Syria where lawful) |
| GCC | Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman |
| Maghreb | Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, (Libya) |
| UK & Ireland | UK, Ireland |
| Western Europe | Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Scandinavia |
| North America | USA, Canada |
| Rest of world | All other countries (open sign-up, regional pool = worldwide until local density exists) |

### Hub cities (launch focus)
**MENA:** Cairo, Alexandria, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Kuwait City, Amman, Casablanca
**West:** London, Birmingham, Manchester, Berlin/Frankfurt (Rhine-Main), Paris, Toronto, New York/New Jersey, Chicago, Houston, Detroit/Dearborn

(Prioritise by waitlist data; the list is capped at ~16–20 for spend focus.)

## 2. Pre-launch (Months 0–6, in parallel with the MVP build)

- **Global waitlist** (AR/EN, French landing page too): Halal Mode explainer + the "What kind of spouse are you?" compatibility quiz (shareable result). Waitlist entries record country/city so we know which countries can open as **Open** on day one. **Target: 150k waitlist worldwide, with ≥ 2,000 in each hub city.**
- **Shariah Advisory Board:** 4–6 scholars from different schools and regions (Egypt, the Gulf, the Maghreb, UK/Europe, North America); a published charter; quarterly reviews.
- **Advisors:** marriage counsellor, psychologist, family lawyers (Egypt, UAE, UK, US), trust & safety expert.
- **Community partnerships in every hub city:** mosques, Islamic centres, MSAs/ISOCs on university campuses, women's study circles. Marriage seminars ("Choosing a spouse in the modern age") with QR sign-ups.
- **Women-first seeding everywhere:** female community leaders and creators, safety-led messaging (verified men only, photo privacy, guardian option).
- **Legal (heavier for a global launch):** holding company (e.g. UAE ADGM/DIFC or UK) + local entities where needed (Egypt for local payments); trademark "HALAL MEET" + the Arabic mark in all 17 launch countries (Madrid Protocol where possible); a multi-jurisdiction DPIA; country-by-country legal opinions on matchmaking rules, mixed-gender events, data localisation (KSA, Egypt, UAE, Qatar have specific cross-border transfer rules); app-store dating policy review per storefront.
- **Localisation:** Arabic + English at launch; **French in month 2** (France, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Belgium). German, Turkish and Urdu follow based on demand data.

## 3. Global launch playbook

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| -6 | Closed beta: 2,000 users across 6 hub cities (Cairo, Riyadh, Dubai, London, Toronto, Berlin), gender-balanced; daily feedback, safety drills, payment tests in every storefront |
| -3 | Press kit in AR/EN/FR; creator previews in each region; scholar videos on values (not a "fatwa on the app") |
| -1 | Waitlist "early access" invites roll out by country, gender-balanced |
| **0** | **Global launch:** app live in all storefronts, Passport live, Founding Members programme open. Countries meeting the density threshold launch as **Open**; others launch as **Growing** (regional pools) |
| +1 to +4 | City activations in hub cities: **Halal Meet Evenings** (family-friendly seminar + verified singles event), adapted per country (family-attended or gender-separate formats where appropriate) |
| +4 | First country status reviews: flip Growing → Open where thresholds are met; announce locally ("Halal Meet is now fully open in Jordan") as a PR moment |
| +6 | Ramadan / Eid / Summer Homecoming campaign, whichever falls next |
| +8 | Founding Members conversion campaign begins 3 weeks before their free period ends (clear reminders, no surprise charges) |
| +12 | First success-story programme ("families introduced" stories first; engagements follow) |

**Messaging pillars (global):**
1. *"Serious people only."* (verification + mandatory intention)
2. *"Know why you match."* (explainable compatibility)
3. *"Your family, your way."* (Halal Mode + guardian)
4. *"Safe by design."* (privacy, anti-scam)

**Passport corridors to market from day one:** Egypt ↔ GCC, UK ↔ Egypt/Pakistan-heritage communities (as Urdu arrives), France ↔ Maghreb, North America ↔ Egypt/Levant/GCC, Germany ↔ Turkey/Levant. Run the **Summer Homecoming** Travel-mode campaign every June–August.

## 8. Key risks & mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Thin local pools from launching everywhere at once** | Empty feeds → day-one churn | Hub-city focus, Growing-country regional pools, Founding Members Passport, honest end-of-pool states, gender-balanced queues |
| Gender imbalance (typically more men) | Infinite feed amplifies it: men get no replies, women are flooded → churn | Women-first acquisition, feed exposure & inbound pacing, verified-only defaults, onboarding queue, quality floor |
| Operational overload (moderation/support in many time zones & languages) | Slow responses, safety incidents | 24/7 follow-the-sun moderation partner, automation-first triage, P0 SLA < 1 h everywhere, staged storefront rollout possible within launch week if needed |
| Regulatory restrictions in specific countries | Launch blocks / takedown | Legal opinions before launch per country; ability to geo-disable features (events, video) per country; ship without a country rather than delay the whole launch |
| Perceived as "just another dating app" by families/scholars | Brand rejection | Advisory Board, guardian features, ethical monetization, no-boost policy, respectful marketing |
| Religious controversy (sect, madhhab, interpretation) | Backlash | Neutral choice architecture, no rulings, optional fields, Advisory Board diversity |
| Scams/romance fraud (higher with Passport live at launch) | Harm, reputation | ID required for Passport, cross-border risk signals, in-chat warnings, "never send money" education |
| App store policy (dating category, payments) in many storefronts | Rejection, margin | Follow guidelines (18+, moderation, reporting), IAP for digital goods, web for human services where allowed |
| Data breach of religious/sensitive data | Severe | Field-level encryption, minimisation, pen tests, residency, incident response plan |
| Arabic brand-name mistranslation ("ميت" = dead) | Ridicule | Use «لقاء حلال» or the Latin wordmark; test with focus groups |
| Founding Members churn when free period ends | Revenue dip | Value reminders, annual-plan offer, honest pre-expiry notices |
| Success = churn | LTV cap | Engagement Mode, marketplace, referrals, couples' courses |
